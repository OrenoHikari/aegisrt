# Abstract
REC-Bench studies compositional language and counting under distribution shift.

# Introduction
PROBLEM: Existing datasets use incompatible language templates and do not expose cross-dataset generalization.

# Method
METHOD: REC-Bench standardizes compositional query types and evaluates region and density baselines on controlled shifts.
CONTRIBUTION: The benchmark separates attribute, relation, and negation queries and publishes a shared evaluation protocol.
DATASET: REC-Bench
METRIC: MAE, exact-count accuracy, calibration error

# Results
RESULT: All evaluated baselines lose at least 18 percent exact-count accuracy under relational distribution shift.

# Limitations
LIMITATION: REC-Bench contains synthetic query balancing and may not represent naturally occurring user language.
