# Abstract
A region-grounding architecture connects referring expressions to countable visual regions.

# Introduction
PROBLEM: Existing visual counting systems do not reliably bind compositional referring expressions to the intended object regions.

# Method
METHOD: Grounded Region Counting first predicts language-aligned regions and then aggregates region-level count estimates.
CONTRIBUTION: The method separates linguistic grounding from numerical aggregation so that each stage can be inspected.
DATASET: REC-Count, RefCOCO-Count
METRIC: MAE, RMSE

# Results
RESULT: Grounded Region Counting reduces MAE from 12.4 to 9.1 on REC-Count under the paper's evaluation protocol.

# Limitations
LIMITATION: Region proposal errors propagate directly into the final count.
